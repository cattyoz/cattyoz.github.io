A few good things to know

01 / THE HOURLY RESET

Fresh sets on the hour.

Each grade and subject has a time-based question set. Reloading won’t create a new set or erase submitted answers. At the next hour, a new practice set loads. An active self-test keeps its original questions until you finish.

02 / THE LEARNING LEVELS

K–12, with room to grow.

Math progresses from counting to introductory calculus. Science, language, and logic use grade bands. High-school math pathways vary; these are sample topics, not a standards-aligned or complete school curriculum.

03 / YOUR OWN DEVICE

No accounts. No uploads.

Your name, notes, tasks, and results stay in this browser. Clearing browser data can remove them. Export a backup regularly. Hourly sets follow this device’s clock; they do not use a server.
